## Corrected assessment after investigation

The Owner asked for an investigation and a plain explanation before deciding
how to proceed. This investigation was read-only; it did not dispatch a
candidate or request a certificate.

### What the earlier hold got wrong

The current [protected-parent plan](../mvp-protected-log-parent-2026-09-19.md)
requires a reviewed two-operation CA budget. Neither it nor the
[MVP procedure](../mvp-live-acceptance.md) makes a successful fresh crt.sh lookup or
complete audit of every historical certificate a mandatory gate.

The continuation assessment missed the later
[September 21 capacity review](v3.1.76-readiness-and-live-plan-2026-09-21.md#capacity-assessment-recent-issuance-not-thirteen-detailed-audits).
That review reconciled the three relevant recent certificate fingerprints and
explicitly rejected a new requirement to audit the older ten records. Carrying
the earlier partial-audit diagnosis forward, then treating a failed supplemental
lookup as a mandatory blocker, was an assessment error. That historical GO is
not reused as authorization for this candidate; its underlying receipts were
independently checked against new observations.
