## Original failure and confirmed cause

The collector opened `mvp-install` at **09:00:33 UTC**, with its original
09:30:33 deadline. The observation started at 09:01:49. The normal moving-HTTPS
installer, delivered through the reviewed active qualification gateway, ran
once at **09:01:53–09:01:55 UTC** and returned exit 1:

```text
SOFTWARE-LIFECYCLE-INSTALL-RELEASE-REFUSED
```

Stderr was empty. SBXR remained Not installed. None of the five required
`mvp-install` checks was recorded as observed, and its draft was never finished.
No installer retry, product repair, additional certificate operation or Karing
import followed.

Read-only diagnosis established the mismatch in
[`cmd/sbxr-release/bootstrap.go`, lines 411–412 at the tested commit](https://github.com/albertloky/SBXR/blob/a56e7c28e11ea4ee6f84cbceb826376774cafacb/cmd/sbxr-release/bootstrap.go#L411-L412).
The generated shell installer admits schema-2
indexes only with `first-subscription-clean-install` or
`subscription-clean-install-repair`, both with an empty source list. The signed
candidate correctly declares `recurring-subscription-upgrade` with the exact
v3.1.81 source and `sbxr-subscription-update-v1` contract. The Go updater accepts
that format; the generated installer does not.

Gateway-downloaded installer and index bytes matched the signed assets. The
unchanged packaged index-validation expression was extracted and exercised
locally, without executing the installer again:

- The actual signed recurring index was rejected (exit 1).
- A diagnostic clean-install-repair/empty-source control was accepted (exit 0).
- Unknown-scope and empty-recurring-source controls were rejected (exit 1).

The controls were parser inputs only, not trusted releases or live evidence.
At the tested commit, the generated-installer regression covered clean-install
repair but not a recurring index with its nonempty source list. This explains
why native verification and the packaged upgrade passed while fresh installation
failed. The parser predates the preceding menu-deadline repair; no claim is made
that the latter introduced this defect.

The confirmed defect and bounded repair criteria are recorded in
[#358](https://github.com/albertloky/SBXR/issues/358). **No implementation fix or
passing fix regression was part of this stopped attempt.** The issue required
testing the actual generated installer/index boundary before another candidate,
while preserving existing format, identity, source, contract, archive and
executable checks. This separate defect ended the bounded repair/qualification cycle under
the [implementation guide](../../agents/implementation.md).
