The tracked [`mvp-operator-run.py`](../../../.github/scripts/mvp-operator-run.py)
reserves `LABEL.operator.stdout` and `LABEL.operator.stderr` with exclusive
creation and mode `0600`. It refuses existing outer logs or the same label's
inner `.private`, `.stderr` and `.receipt.json` captures, including broken links,
before execution. It execs the supplied command, preserving stdin, cwd, exit
status and cancellation without adding a child supervisor. A log reserved
before a reservation error remains for inspection.
